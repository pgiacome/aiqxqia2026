\section*{Step 3 --- Rebuild the zip with the form included}
The zip must contain the LaTeX sources with \texttt{main.tex} at the top
level, the final PDF and the signed form. From the repository root:

\begin{Verbatim}[frame=single,fontsize=\small]
cd papers/aiqxqia2026/paper14-camera-ready
rm -f paper14-camera-ready.zip
zip paper14-camera-ready.zip main.tex references.bib main.bbl \
    ceurart.cls elsarticle-num-names.bst cc-by.pdf main.pdf \
    copyright-form-signed.pdf
unzip -l paper14-camera-ready.zip     # expect 8 files
\end{Verbatim}
